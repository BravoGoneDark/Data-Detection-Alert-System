TITLE: Autonomous Zero-Trust Threat Intelligence Whitepaper
AUTHORS: Cyber Defense Architecture Group, DDAS SOC Labs
CLASSIFICATION: RESTRICTED

ABSTRACT:
Recent advances in distributed denial-of-service and credential stuffing attacks require continuous zero-trust behavioral analysis. Content-addressable storage combined with Locality-Sensitive Hashing allows forensic teams to identify duplicate binary streams in constant O(1) retrieval time. Our multi-tier quarantine engine intercepts unauthorized exfiltration bursts and computes sliding-window download velocity thresholds to isolate compromised accounts.
