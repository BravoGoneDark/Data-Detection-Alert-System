SUMMARY OF RESEARCH: Threat Intelligence & Zero-Trust Architecture Review
RESEARCH TEAM: DDAS Cyber Defense Laboratory
CLEARANCE: RESTRICTED

SYNOPSIS:
Distributed denial-of-service surges and credential stuffing threats mandate zero-trust forensic analysis. Utilizing content-addressable storage alongside Locality-Sensitive Hashing enables security incident teams to evaluate duplicate binary streams with rapid O(1) candidate pruning. Furthermore, our quarantine engine dynamically mitigates exfiltration bursts by calculating sliding-window download velocity thresholds to isolate compromised accounts.
